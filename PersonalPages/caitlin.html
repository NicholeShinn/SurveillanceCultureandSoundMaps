NOT SPOTS.


Exposition.

Poor service. Fractured connectivity. NOT SPOTS is an interactive sound map of locations within Detroit city limits where I experience "spotty" or non-existant data and Wi-Fi service. This is an exploration of lack of resources, coverage quality, and surveillance.


Hover over me!



"Location 1, Belle Isle."

Crickets, cicadas, barges, airplanes, waves, motor boats, walking, biking, dogs, gravel crunching, birds chirping, overheard conversation -- the earth responding to itself.
