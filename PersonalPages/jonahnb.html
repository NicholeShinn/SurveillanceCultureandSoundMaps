MASPETH JUNK SOUND



"Listen to a roadway - eyes closed - distinguish size shape make of car by the sound - also speed and health of engine."

this sound map builds on the urban mediatation text score created by pauline oliveiros and aims to develop a protocol for engaged listening in an area of polluted sound.

does a schoolbus sound different to a box truck? does a driver for a grocery distributer wholesaler handle the road surface more or less deftly than an amazon driver? what does relative silence sound like in a space like this, and what steps are necessary take to hear it?

Listen to a roadway - eyes closed - distinguish size shape make of car by the sound - also speed and health of engine. consider their origin and terminus, their relation to our daily lives, and the costs of their freedom of movement.
