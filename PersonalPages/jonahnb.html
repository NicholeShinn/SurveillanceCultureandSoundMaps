MASPETH JUNK SOUND



"Listen to a roadway - eyes closed - distinguish size shape make of car by the sound - also speed and health of engine."



Variously referred to as Field, Folding, Missionary, or Chaplain Organs, these portable reed organs were made to be transported to remote locations and typically used by missionaries, military clergy, tent revivalists, they also served as a portable home entertainment. Listening to the extended playing of a single chord, even an sonically difficult chord, creates a space where sounds build and tones collide. Resolution comes through the listener's own mind sorting out the wave patterns.
