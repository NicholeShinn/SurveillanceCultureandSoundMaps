MASPETH JUNK SOUND



"Listen to a roadway - eyes closed - distinguish size shape make of car by the sound - also speed and health of engine."

this sound map builds on the urban mediatation text score created by pauline oliveiros to develop a protocol for engaged listening in an area of polluted sound. within this radius of the maspeth industiral business zone, we sitaute ourselves within the sonic arguments of industrial and residential priorities, of transient and permanenent sound within natural and built space.

the sizes and shapes, speeds and healths of vehicles in the roadways in this enviornment allow us to imagine the purposes they serve. does a schoolbus sound different to a box truck? does a driver for a grocery distributer wholesaler handle the road surface more or less deftly than an amazon driver? how often de we hear bikes compared to compact vehicles compared to industrial freight?

Listen to a roadway - eyes closed - distinguish size shape make of car by the sound - also speed and health of engine. consider their origin and terminus, their relation to our daily lives, and the costs of their freedom of movement. what does "relative" silence sound like in a space like this, and what steps are necessary take to hear it?



to explore the sonic qualities of the locations selected for this project, i have attempted to add a harmonic layer to dissonant industrial environments. this is done through maniuplating pedal notes drawn from the estey field organ tone archive. originally, these pedal organs were used for field missions, schools, small chapels, military programs, and home use. this project uses estey's archive of resonant tones as an underlying texture within field recordings, programmed to distort when recordings peak over a certain threshold.
