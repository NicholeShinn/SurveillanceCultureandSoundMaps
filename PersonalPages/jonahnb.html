I WAS HERE IN MASPETH

this sound map draws inspiration from the urban mediatation text score created by pauline oliveiros and aims to develop a protocol for engaged listening in an area of polluted sound.

what does relative silence sound like in such a space, and what steps are necessary take to hear it?

Listen to a roadway - eyes closed - distinguish size shape make of car by the sound - also speed and health of engine. consider their origin and terminus. when they are gone, listen for their echoes in your environment.



field organs and relative quiet in maspeth

to explore the sonic qualities of the locations selected for this project, i have attempted to add a harmonic layer to dissonant industrial environments. this is done through maniuplating pedal notes drawn from the estey field organ tone archive.

originally, these pedal organs were used for field missions, schools, small chapels, military programs, and home use. the archive describes the its collection of sustained notes as historic means through which people have connected with the sound waves of their environments. "resolution," the archive writes, "comes through the listener's own mind sorting out the wave patterns."

this project uses estey's archive of resonant tones as an underlying texture within field recordings, automated to distort when recordings peak over a threshold speculatively determined to be the "noise floor" of each location.
